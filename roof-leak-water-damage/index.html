Roof Leak Water Damage in Springfield: What to Know

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. Rain slips under a lifted shingle, a cracked flashing joint or a tired skylight seal, then runs along the decking and rafters before it drops onto the insulation. By the time a brown ring shows on the ceiling, the attic above it has often been wet for a while. Locating all of the moisture and drying it is our job.

We are a water damage crew, not a roofing company. Inside the home, our team takes out saturated insulation, dries the wood and drywall, treats any mold and repairs the ceiling. Should the leak still be active, our crew can cover that section with a tarp for now, or time our work with the roofing contractor you pick so nothing gets sealed over an open leak.

Do This First

What to Do Right Now

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Contain the WaterPut a pan or bucket beneath each drip, and shift rugs, electronics and furniture clear of the wet area. Lay towels or plastic sheeting down to protect the floor.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingA ceiling that sags with trapped water can be drained: stay off to the side and make a small opening at its lowest spot, with a bucket underneath.
  4. Stay Off the RoofDo not climb onto a wet roof or crawl through a dark attic. From indoors, mark where the water enters, and let professionals handle everything else.
  5. Document What You SeeBefore anything is moved or discarded, get video and photos of the attic, the ceiling and damaged items, and then phone us so drying can begin.
The Usual Culprits

How It Usually Starts

  • Aging, cracked seals around a skylight frame that no longer keep water out.
  • Gaps at chimneys, vents and sidewalls where the flashing has separated or rusted through.
  • Leaves and grit collecting where two roof slopes meet, damming rain until it seeps through.
  • Fasteners working loose over time, so water follows the nail shank into the attic.
  • A vent pipe boot whose rubber has split from long sun exposure.
If It Is Left

Where the Damage Spreads

Ceiling Drywall

A ceiling panel left damp will discolor, bow and weaken. Seams split, the tape lets go, and a heavy enough section can drop with little warning.

Attic Insulation

Wet insulation mats down, stops insulating and holds water against the ceiling drywall for days. Fiberglass can sometimes be dried. Removal is the usual answer for wet cellulose.

Inside the Walls

From the attic, a leak can drop into a stud bay and soak the wall insulation and drywall from behind. Bubbling paint or a swollen baseboard may be the only sign.

Hidden Attic Mold

Mold does well in an attic that is warm, dark and full of damp wood and drywall paper. Within 24 to 48 hours it can start growing, then move across the bottom of the roof deck.

Our Process

How We Handle Roof Leak Water Damage

1

Map Where Water Went

Using thermal imaging and moisture meters, our team checks wall cavities, ceilings and the attic and charts the whole route the water took, even where the room below shows nothing.

2

Tarp and Protect

An exposed roof gets covered with a temporary tarp, or we line things up with your roofing contractor. Floors and belongings below the wet ceiling are covered as well.

3

Tear Out and Dry

Unsalvageable drywall and insulation come out, and dehumidifiers and air movers go into the attic space and each room under it, with readings taken until the framing has dried.

4

Where it is called for, we apply an antimicrobial to the bare framing. After the roofer has finished, new insulation goes in, fresh drywall is hung and finished, and the ceiling is primed and painted to match.

Coverage

Does Insurance Cover It?

Homeowners policies often cover interior water damage when a roof leak is sudden and accidental, such as wind tearing off shingles or a limb puncturing the roof. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

Pricing

What Affects the Cost

  • How long the roof leaked before it was found, and any mold growth.
  • How many square feet of wall and ceiling drywall have to be removed.
  • Whether water got past the ceiling into walls, flooring or belongings below.
  • The amount of soaked insulation in the attic, and if it must come out.
